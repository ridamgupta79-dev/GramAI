Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY)

A scheme launched by the Hon’ble Prime Minister to “fund the unfunded” by bringing micro-enterprises into the formal financial system and extending affordable credit to them.

check_circle

Eligibility Criteria

  • arrow_right Any Indian Citizen
  • arrow_right Has a business plan for a non-farm income generating activity (manufacturing, processing, trading, or service sector).
  • arrow_right Credit need less than ₹10 Lakhs.

help_center Frequently Asked Questions

What is the interest rate? expand_more
Interest rates are decided by lending institutions as per RBI guidelines. It typically varies from 8% to 12% depending on the bank and applicant profile.
Are there any processing fees? expand_more
No processing fee is charged for Shishu loans (up to ₹50,000). For Kishore and Tarun, a nominal fee may be charged by the bank.
